# Code of Conduct

[English](#code-of-conduct) · [中文](#行为准则)

## Our standard

AdMind welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Project spaces should remain useful, respectful and focused on improving the product and its evidence.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- separating disagreement about an idea from judgment of a person;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting inaccurate claims;
- respecting privacy, copyright and confidential information; and
- helping maintain a safe environment for contributors and users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, repeated personal attacks and disruption that prevents productive collaboration.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, issue warnings or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repeated behavior and the safety of affected participants.

# 行为准则

## 我们的标准

AdMind 欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者进行建设性合作。项目空间应保持有用、尊重，并聚焦改进产品及其证据。

我们期待参与者提供具体可执行的反馈，把对观点的分歧与对个人的评价分开，承认不确定性并纠正不准确信息，尊重隐私、版权和保密内容，并帮助维持安全环境。

不可接受的行为包括骚扰、威胁、歧视性语言、性化关注、故意泄露私人信息、反复人身攻击，以及阻碍正常协作的破坏行为。

## 适用范围

本标准适用于仓库的 Issue、Pull Request、Discussion、评审和其他项目空间，也适用于个人代表本项目进行公开活动的场景。

## 执行

请私下向仓库所有者报告行为问题，不要在公开 Issue 中包含敏感个人信息。维护者可以编辑或移除贡献、锁定讨论、发出警告或限制违反本标准的参与者。

执行决定应综合考虑上下文、严重程度、是否重复发生和受影响参与者的安全。
