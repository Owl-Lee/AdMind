# Product evidence and claims policy

## Evidence already gathered

- 9 qualitative interviews across peers and parents.
- 8 of 9 interviewees leaned toward a single consolidated ad break when an ad is unavoidable.
- Repeated pain points included interruption at urgent or emotionally intense moments, accidental navigation, loss of comments/danmaku/progress controls, and poor contextual relevance.
- A separate research package contains a 43-paper peer-reviewed evidence matrix.

These interviews are directional discovery evidence, not a representative survey. The product UI labels the sample size and does not turn it into a population claim.

## S1 hypothesis

If a guaranteed campaign must be delivered, moving it from a high-tension fixed point to the next safe transition and selecting an approved short muted creative should lower perceived disruption while preserving the commercial commitment.

This is a hypothesis until an A/B test measures outcomes such as completion, abandonment, accidental click, ad recall, revenue and contract delivery.

## Public asset policy

The public repository uses fictional brands and original CSS artwork. Real platform screenshots remain private research evidence and are not shipped as default public assets.
