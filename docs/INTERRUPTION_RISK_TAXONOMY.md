# Long-form video interruption-risk taxonomy

AdMind does not equate low motion with a safe advertising opportunity. A calm image can still be emotionally intense, narratively essential, or dependent on uninterrupted continuity.

## Three independent axes

1. **Emotional intensity** — affective weight such as fear, intimacy, grief, relief, joy, humiliation, or anger.
2. **Narrative criticality** — how necessary the moment is for understanding a reveal, decision, relationship change, or payoff.
3. **Interruption risk** — the expected damage caused by inserting an advertisement at that exact interval.

## High-risk continuity classes

| Class | Long-form examples | Why motion alone fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical danger | fights, pursuit, injury, medical urgency | Often high-motion and obvious, but recovery can remain sensitive after motion drops. |
| Death and grief | death reveal, funeral, silent mourning | Frequently quiet but emotionally non-interruptible. |
| Romantic intimacy | kiss, embrace, sexual intimacy, proposal | May be visually still while carrying a major relationship payoff. |
| Relationship turning points | confession, reunion, farewell, sacrifice | Dialogue and reaction shots matter more than motion. |
| Suspense and revelation | culprit reveal, hidden clue, pre-jump-scare pause, plot twist | Silence and stillness can be the mechanism that creates tension. |
| Emotional confrontation | argument, breakdown, apology, reconciliation | Interrupting breaks emotional continuity and character understanding. |
| Critical dialogue | diagnosis, testimony, instructions, exposition, verdict | Calm talking can contain indispensable information. |
| Performance continuity | song, dance, comedy setup/punchline, magic routine | Value depends on rhythm and an uninterrupted setup-to-payoff sequence. |
| Decisive competition | final goal, match point, finish line, judging result | The key moment can be brief and must not be covered. |
| Vulnerability and trauma | children at risk, disaster, self-harm, abuse aftermath | Requires both policy protection and conservative uncertainty handling. |

## Allow criteria

An `ALLOW` recommendation requires all of the following:

- low interruption risk;
- low narrative criticality;
- a clear shot, chapter, dialogue, or emotional-recovery boundary;
- no protected or sensitive context;
- enough remaining playback time for the complete approved creative.

`DELAY` means less harmful but still imperfect. `BLOCK` means do not execute at that time. `UNCERTAIN` means evidence is missing or repeated runs disagree; AdMind then rejects fullscreen insertion by default.
